The Unusual World

Strange, true facts about our planet in under a minute.

The Unusual World makes short videos about surprising places, borders, history and science. Every fact is checked against reliable sources before a video is made.

Uploader app

"Unusual World Uploader" is a private tool the channel owner uses to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.
